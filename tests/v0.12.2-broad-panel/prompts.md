# Igni broad-scope LLM panel — "thoughts on Igni"

## 1. Thoughts on Igni

> You're about to read the README, architecture document, language spec
> (v0.12.2), and beginner tutorial (v2.5) for **Igni**, a small research-stage
> programming language that targets Flutter.
>
> Read all four end-to-end. Then share your honest, detailed thoughts.
> Structure your response with these five sections:
>
> 1. **One-paragraph summary** — what Igni is, who it's for, and what makes
>    it different, in your own words.
> 2. **What works** — the design decisions you think are smart. Be specific
>    (reference spec sections, tutorial parts, concrete rules).
> 3. **What worries you** — the decisions, omissions, or gaps that feel
>    risky for adoption, learnability, or correctness. Be specific.
> 4. **Comparisons** — what existing language or framework is Igni most
>    like (SwiftUI, Elm, Jetpack Compose, Flutter, Roc, etc.)? Where does
>    Igni have an edge? Where does it lose?
> 5. **What you'd change** — one or two specific, scoped changes you'd
>    push for if you joined the project. Things that could ship in the
>    next few versions, not speculative features.
>
> Be honest. If something confuses you, say so directly with a quote. If a
> design feels wrong, say why.
